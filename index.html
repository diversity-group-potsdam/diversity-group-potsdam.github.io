About

The Data & Systems Lab is part of the Department of Computer Science at University Name. Led by Prof. Jane Doe, our group studies the design and evaluation of systems that handle large-scale, real-world data — from infrastructure to the algorithms running on top of it. We collaborate closely with industry and other academic labs, and we're always excited to work with curious, motivated students.

Research Areas

Distributed Systems

Scalable storage, consensus, and fault-tolerant infrastructure for modern data-intensive applications.

Applied Machine Learning

Practical ML methods for structured and unstructured data, with a focus on robustness and efficiency.

Data Management

Query processing, indexing, and data quality for large, heterogeneous, and evolving datasets.

Human-Centered Evaluation

Benchmarks and user studies that measure how systems perform for the people who actually use them.
