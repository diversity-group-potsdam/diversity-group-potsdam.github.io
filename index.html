About

The Diversity Group Potsdam is part of the Diversity in Education and Development group at the University of Potsdam, led by Prof. Dr. Linda Juang. We study how cultural diversity, migration, and discrimination shape school belonging, social cohesion in multicultural settings, and the identity development and adjustment of adolescents and young adults with immigrant backgrounds. We collaborate with researchers across Europe and beyond, and in 2021 hosted the 3rd Cultural Diversity, Migration, and Education (CDME) Conference with 176 participants from 27 countries.

Research Areas

Acculturation & Ethnic-Racial Identity

Identity development, discrimination, and psychological and sociocultural adjustment among adolescents and young adults of immigrant background.

School Belonging & Diversity Climate

How diversity climate, norms, and practices in schools relate to students' sense of belonging and adjustment.

Social Cohesion in Multicultural Settings

Intergroup relations, interethnic friendships, and intercultural competence in multiethnic classrooms.

Migration & Education

How migration and cultural diversity contribute to children's educational experiences and outcomes.
